About Me

Where my story meets scholarship

Community Roots

Growing Together with Community

Growing up in Busan, Korea, within diverse communities and neighborhoods that were destinations for immigrant families, shaped my earliest sense of belonging and resilience. These experiences became the roots of my interest in migration, inequality, and the policies that shape people’s lives.

Curiosity Exploration

Curiosity as a Guiding Force

Curiosity has always been more than simple wonder—it was a way of exploring the world around me. Each question opened new paths, and this spirit of exploration continues to guide my research and scholarship.

Stories Scholarship

Exploring Stories through Scholarship

Books opened new horizons, but it was the stories of people’s lives that truly shaped my path. Scholarship has become the way I could weave lived experiences with critical inquiry, grounding my work on migration and inequality in both evidence and empathy.

Greetings,

I grew up in Busan, Korea, in a neighborhood that was home to more than twenty immigrant families. I watched exclusion shape their opportunities in school, in health care, and in everyday life. As some families moved on, new families arrived and met the same barriers. I also saw the resilience they brought to building community and starting over. Later, as a social worker at a child welfare center in Busan, I assessed families’ needs and connected them to services, and saw how much depended on whether those systems were within reach.

These experiences shape the questions I ask as a researcher: how immigration policy, the social safety net, and digital technologies support or constrain the economic security of immigrant and refugee families. I draw on quantitative, qualitative, and computational methods, and I aim for research that informs policy and practice.

My Key Interests

Core themes guiding my research

Economic Integration

How immigrants and refugees build economic security after arrival, through work, income, and household resources.

Immigration Policy & the Safety Net

How immigration and social policies open or close access to support for immigrant and refugee families.

Digital Inclusion & AI

When digital skills, online platforms, and AI tools in human services become stepping-stones, and when they become barriers.

Community Engagement

Bridging research and practice through partnerships with local organizations.
