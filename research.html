    PROJECTS

    Forced Migration Initiative (Research Assistant, 2023–present)

    The Forced Migration Initiative at Washington University in St. Louis leads research, education, and training to improve the quality of life and well-being of people who have experienced forced migration. Projects include multidimensional economic integration of newly resettled Afghans (Russell Sage Foundation), restrictive migration policies and the well-being of highly educated immigrants, and ethnic networks and health disparities among Afghan refugees.
